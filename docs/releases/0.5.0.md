Postio 0.5.0 is an alpha, and the first release with a Mac app to download. It is the release where Postio Focus — the one dense inbox, and the four things it does to mail — became the app on both platforms, over one engine.

- Postio Focus is the desktop app, on Linux and the Mac: one list of mail as it arrived; invitations, direct questions and to-dos called out on their rows with the sentence that raised them; digests on a cadence you choose; Filtered, with its reason on every row and one key to restore it, and now multi-select there as in any folder.
- The native macOS app is released: `postio-0.5.0-macos-arm64.zip`, Apple silicon, macOS 14 or newer. It is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so open it once with right-click → Open.
- Search moves with you: a dropdown as you type, facets for sender, date and more, and a results view with Files and People tabs, Quick Look and save.
- Contacts, on both platforms: the people in your mail, editable, mergeable, with vCard import and export.
- An MCP server lets an agent you connect read the mail you share with it, and archive, move and label it. Settings shows each agent's access and revokes it. Nothing is shared until you share it.
- The reader paints a sender's own page on paper, or the message in the app's colours; zoom, find in the message, darken a sheet, and fall back visibly when a render runs past its deadline.
- Notifications on the Mac follow `[sync] notify_roles`, as on Linux: mail arriving in Trash, Spam or Archive no longer notifies.
- Retrieval is sturdier: a moved message keeps its body and is not shown twice, a header-only refresh keeps the body already fetched, a refused download says so at once, a message open while the connection drops loads as soon as it returns, and an account signed in again asks its new connection for what was waiting.
- Under the hood: mail-parser 1.0 (damaged base64 recovered instead of stored as text, RFC 2231 names and encoded words decoded across their pieces, a date's `+5` read as +05:00) and mail-builder 2.0 (control characters can no longer inject a header through an address); Turso 0.8.3 with its full-text index on tantivy 0.27; Rust 1.99.1.
