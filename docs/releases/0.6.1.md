Postio 0.6.1 is an alpha. It brings back threads: a conversation's messages can be seen and walked again, on the Mac and on Linux alike, and Filtered's tabs sit where the inbox's strip does.

- Threads in the list: Right expands a conversation in place to show its messages, and Left folds it again. Its row names who took part ("Lena, Ben, you"), how many messages it holds and how many are new. Filtered and search results expand the same way.
- Threads in the message window: the thread bar shows each message as a chip, { and } go to the first and the latest message, g u to the first unread, and a thread opens at its first unread message. alt+t lists the thread's outline, and alt+period opens the thread menu.
- A reply that arrives while a thread is open shows up live, and a snoozed thread wakes when someone replies to it.
- Archive, snooze, labels and mute act on the whole thread, and the footer says what each key will act on. Read state and markers stay per message, and alt+v and alt+Delete move or delete just the one message.
- m mutes a thread, and alt+shift+s splits a message and its replies into a thread of their own, which Postio remembers. Both can be undone.
- alt+e replies to the latest message. Forwarding asks what to send: this message (1), the whole thread (2), or its attachments (3).
- Search answers in threads and opens a thread at the message that matched.
- Mail is grouped by subject only within seven days, so unrelated messages that share a subject no longer join one thread.
- Filtered's reason tabs are the same square-cornered chip as Has action, in a row the height of the inbox's strip, and its day headings match the inbox's, so the list no longer jumps when you switch between the two views.
